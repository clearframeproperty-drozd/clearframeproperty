What this service is. ClearFrame Property Records provides visual documentation of accessible and visible property conditions. It is not a home inspection, repair estimate, code-compliance inspection, or legal determination of responsibility.

We do not determine who caused any damage, and we do not calculate security-deposit deductions. Those decisions remain the responsibility of the property owner or manager.

California Rental Documentation Has Changed

California Civil Code §1950.5 includes photographic documentation requirements for applicable residential tenancies and security-deposit deductions. Our service helps property owners create an organized visual record, but landlords remain responsible for legal deadlines, notices, deposit accounting and compliance decisions.

Access to the property

The client booking a visit is responsible for arranging access to the property at the scheduled time. If an area is locked or otherwise inaccessible, it will be noted in the report rather than photographed.
