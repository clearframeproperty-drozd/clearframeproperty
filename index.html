The problem with phone photos

Photos Are Easy to Take. Reliable Documentation Is Not.

  • Photos end up scattered across different phones
  • No one can say exactly when or where a photo was taken
  • General overview shots are missing
  • That one wall or closet never gets photographed
  • Move-In and Move-Out photos can't be properly compared
  • A year later, no one remembers the original condition
  • The landlord loses hours sorting through files
  • The tenant never gets a clear confirmation of the unit's condition

We turn an entire property into one organized, easy-to-review visual record.

Optional LiDAR & 3D capture

See the Property as a Space — Not Just a Collection of Photos.

iPhone LiDAR and Polycam add a walkable 3D model on top of the standard photo report — useful for remote reviews, spatial context, and understanding how a room actually fits together.

The 3D scan supplements the photographic report. It does not replace the required high-resolution photographs. Mobile LiDAR measurements are approximate and are not survey-grade measurements.
