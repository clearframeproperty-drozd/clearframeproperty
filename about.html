About

Rental documentation, done the way it should have been all along.

ClearFrame was created to make rental documentation easier, clearer, and more useful than a folder of unorganized phone photos.

Most landlords and tenants already try to document a property with their phone. The problem was never a lack of effort — it's that the results are scattered, unlabeled, and nearly impossible to compare months or years later.

ClearFrame brings a background in professional photography, video production, editing, and visual organization to a task most people are stuck doing themselves. It also brings in modern 3D-capture and AI-assisted tools to keep the process fast without cutting corners on quality.

The result is a service that's local, personal, and detail-oriented — built around getting property owners, managers, realtors, and tenants a record they can actually trust and use.
