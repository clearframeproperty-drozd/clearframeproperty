How it works

Five steps. You're hands-on for exactly one of them.

The client does not need to create, format or organize anything.

01

Choose a Service

Move-In Baseline, Move-Out Pre-Work, Post-Work Documentation, or Complete Rental Record.

02

Select a Date

Pick an available time from our booking calendar — no back-and-forth needed.

03

We Document the Property

A specialist conducts a systematic, room-by-room photo shoot, and 3D scanning if it was requested.

04

We Build the Complete File

We sort the material, label every image, and assemble the finished PDF ourselves.

05

Receive and Share

You get a secure link with your PDF, the original photos, and 3D access if included.

Optional LiDAR & 3D capture

See the Property as a Space — Not Just a Collection of Photos.

iPhone LiDAR and Polycam let us build an additional three-dimensional model of a room or unit on top of the standard photo documentation.

Better spatial orientation

Understand how a space actually connects, not just what's visible in a single frame.

A virtual walk-through

Move through the room the way you would in person, from anywhere.

Layout in context

See exactly where walls, doors and furniture sit relative to each other.

Additional context for photos

The 3D model gives every still photo a clearer spatial reference point.

Convenient remote review

Owners who can't be on site can still review the space in full.

The 3D scan supplements the photographic report. It does not replace the required high-resolution photographs. Mobile LiDAR measurements are approximate and are not survey-grade measurements.
